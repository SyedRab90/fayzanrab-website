# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Silicon Valley founders, early-stage tech executives, and senior people in tech who are high performers and skeptical of fluff. They're time-poor, analytically minded, and have tried every framework and advisory board. They come to this site when they feel something important is stuck — not productively busy, but genuinely uncertain about a decision or direction that matters. The secondary audience is researchers, journalists, and podcast hosts who land on the Research page looking for credibility.

## Product Purpose

Executive coaching practice website for Fayzan Rab MD PCC. The site's one job is to get high-performing founders and tech leaders to reach out. It also establishes Fayzan's credibility through published research, speaking engagements, and client testimonials — so that the decision to reach out feels like a bet worth making.

## Positioning

Fayzan is the only executive coach who is also a psychiatry resident at Emory, an active psychedelic researcher, and has 6+ years coaching Silicon Valley founders. The intersection isn't a tagline — it's the actual mechanism: clinical listening, psychological depth, and direct access to the frontier of consciousness science, applied to leadership. No competitor can truthfully copy this.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from referrals, LinkedIn, podcast mentions, or press coverage. They arrive already pre-qualified — they know who Fayzan is. The site doesn't need to explain coaching; it needs to make the specific case for why Fayzan, and make reaching out feel low-stakes and worth it.

## Product Principles

1. **Reach out is the conversion.** Every design choice is measured against whether it makes a smart, skeptical founder more or less likely to send a message.
2. **Depth earns trust.** The breadth of Fayzan's background (medicine, psychedelics, coaching) is the product. Surface it with specificity, not hype.
3. **Warmth over impressiveness.** The site should feel like a brilliant person you'd want to call, not a brand you'd admire from a distance.
4. **Precision over volume.** Fayzan keeps a small client load. The site should feel select, not promotional.
5. **Real beats decorated.** The actual photos, quotes, publications, and credentials are stronger than any decorative treatment over them.
